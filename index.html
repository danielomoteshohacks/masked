Masked

How much of the hidden detail in your music can you actually hear?

Loud sounds in music hide quieter sounds close to them in pitch and time. This is called masking. Audio formats like MP3 and AAC use a model of an "average" listener to decide which hidden parts can be deleted without anyone noticing.

Masked will run that kind of model on a song you choose. It removes the parts the model says are inaudible, then uses a blind A/B/X listening test to find out whether your ears are more or less sensitive than the model assumes.

or drop an audio file anywhere on this page (WAV, MP3, M4A)

Negative removes less, positive removes more.

This is not a medical hearing test. It measures how you hear one specific kind of audio processing, on your own equipment, in your own room. It cannot diagnose hearing loss. If you have concerns about your hearing, see an audiologist.
