Perceptual listening test

How much of your music can you actually hear?

Loud sounds hide quieter ones close to them in pitch and time. Formats like MP3 and AAC use a model of an "average" listener to delete what it thinks you cannot hear. Masked runs that kind of model on your own song and measures, in a blind test, where your ears sit relative to it.

Start with a song you know well

Drop an audio file here, or choose one. WAV, MP3 or M4A. It stays on your device: nothing is uploaded.

How it works

  1. Analyse. Your song is mapped for sound that the standard model says is masked.
  2. Check. A short tone test confirms you are on headphones.
  3. Test. A blind A/B/X test adjusts how much is removed until you are right about 71% of the time.
  4. Result. Your personal offset from the model, in dB.
